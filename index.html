Grande Prairie · Alberta

Country / Americana / Singer-songwriter

Jon MacDonald is a singer-songwriter from Grande Prairie, Alberta. He mostly plays acoustic guitar, and some piano too. He sings country and Americana, mixing his own originals with songs from artists he loves like Chris Stapleton, Ryan Adams and Fred Eaglesmith. He’s been playing and writing songs since he was a kid, and is now bringing them to the stage.
